Use **fusion-team** and the [swarm runtime](../docs/SWARM_RUNTIME.md) for $ARGUMENTS.
Preserve the selected native roster. Preview `python <plugin-root>/fusion.py
strategies catalog` or `strategies preview --spec FILE`. Generate and optionally
prepare one of the six workflows through `strategies workflow --name NAME --spec
FILE [--prepare --board FILE]`. Preparation calls no providers. Describe actual
dependency/review mechanics and ownership; a topology label is insufficient.
